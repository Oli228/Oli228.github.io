After trip to China for 5 days 6 nights

1. Summary

Honestly, It was absolutely good journey and changed every prejudice on the trip to China. It was moderately clean and I observed many polite youngster who are polite, kind and generous. I even made two Chinese friends from different regions, even though I only had a plan to travel alone but no plan at all to meet any new. Thankfully they approached me when drawing and writing a diary and helped me to explore more local places such as 嶗山(lao shan).

Therefore, I had a wonderful journey in 請島(Qing Dao) and started being more interested in our neighborhood and their culture.

2. Preparation

Items to bring along

1) Mobile applications 📱

  1. A-map: You may already know you cannot use google map or other foreign map application in China. Furthermore, there is the limited information you can search in such applications. It is highly recommended using Amap as you can use the newly introduced translation function in English. It also includes Korean or Japanese.
  2. Alipay: Necessary! better you sign up before your departure. If it is under 200yuan(元), you will not need to pay any extra fee and it is the most convenient method for payment in China.
  3. Trip.com: I started using this app to find accommodations in China as it translated many reviews in the organized format. Also, there are fascinating promotions. If you review some of locations, hotels… , you would get points to spend them to make reservations on the train, tickets and other accommodations.
  4. DiDi: This is the app to call the taxi. I ended up using Amap as it seemed a little cheaper than DiDi. I didn’t compare 1:1, so it is up to you which app to use. When you call Amap, you have to give last two numbers of your phone number in Chinese.

2) Others

  1. Tissues: You will drink a lot of tea there. ;-) So better bring small tissues along with you.
  2. Charger: You will need to use a lot of battery for A-map. Better bringing mobile charger for just in case.
  3. Suncream: In summer, the sun is really strong - better bring sunscream!

3. Daily notes

1) 20th of July in Qing Dao （請 島）

I arrived in 請島(Qing Dao) at night and was frustrated when the hotel made me wait for an hour to pick me up. As it was a free of charge, I tried to be calm but at the end missed the night show which ended at 23:00. My hotel was moderately clean to stay a night but could hear others from the next door.

  • Hotel name: 趣诺思智行酒店（胶东国际机场店）
  • Recommendation: ⭐️⭐️⭐️(very good location and nice employees)
  • Achtung⚠️: better take taxi to go metro as it is quite harsh to walk along.

2) 21st of July in Qing Dao (請島) and Yan Tai(煙台) 🚉

I woke up in the morning and had breakfast in the buffet. Since I booked the train from Qing Dao station(請島站) to Yan Tai station(煙台站) in the afternoon, I walked to Jia Dong stations(膠東站) from hotel.. and found out I cannot walk there. I called DiDi.. fully satisfied.

Since I cannot talk any chinese, I translated “I want to go to Qing Dao station” into chinese and showed it to the staff working in metro. She was the kindest ever and I learnt how to use metro.
I arrived in the station too early, so looked around Silver Fish alley(银魚巷). There are many stores selling coffee, desserts, and adorable items including dolls, diary, … I bought a very special Qing Dao note for my travellernote’s diary. 📔 I wrote and drew during my trip in China -> super satisfied.

I went to the station and there was no line for the foreigner’s entrance. After examining bags, I went to second floor to have lunch in Mcdonald. In the basement floor, you will find other restaurants, supermarket, and cafe. So better check it there as well.

I booked the second class seat with Trip.com and it was the cheapest. Of course you can book it with original website, but it was not easy so decided to pay It was clean enough. I wrote down a daily note and enjoyed the view outside. When taking the train in China as a foreigner, you don’t need a ticket printed and only need your passport.

When arrived in Yan Tai(煙台), I better recommend you taking the taxi to your hotel… I walked all the way till Chao Yang street(朝陽路). (You may walk if you don’t have any bag along. 🎒) All I got from the walk is only the beer.

My second hotel was very satisfying with all aspects including the location, cleanliness, staffs, and the view as well. I took quick nap for a half an hour and walked along the Chao Yang street(朝陽路). As this region is famous for apple, there were many stores selling the juice and small fashion items.

- Hotel name: Lim Hotel - Recommendation: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️(The BEST location ever, kind staff, very clean) - Achtung⚠️: nothing to worry! ah! you should plug in the fridge by yourself.

3) 22nd of July in Yan Tai(煙台)

In the morning, I walked along the ocean, not toward Chao Yang street(朝陽路). The view was outstanding. However there was very strong sun while there was no place to hide.. I started finding any place to have coffee since it was quite exhausting to experience sun that much. At the end, I found the place to have lunch famous among chinese.

I couldn’t understand the menu clearly… After all, I pointed on the bestseller to order. Staffs were saying some sentences but didn’t understand at all :(… What I ordered was the lamb soup which contains its intestine. It was a new experience - I tried it out anyway and it tasted good to be honest. Quite similar to the soup with intestine of cow that we eat in Korea as well.

- Restaurant name: 掖县 朱桥羊湯 - Recommendation: ⭐️⭐️⭐️ - Achtung!: Order the soup with rice or noodle! else you will only have soup...

After lunch, I was still tired and wanted to have some rest. I ordered taxi to get coffee and view Haichang Fisherman’s Pier(海昌渔人码头). At the cafe, I drew some sceneries and wrote diaries. Quite peaceful moment.

- cafe name: More coffee (莫非咖啡) - recommendation: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ - Achtung!: Perfect view! the toilet is super clean! 🧼

I visited small stationery shop near the cafe and bought drawing pens. The shop owner was polite and kind - she was trying to communicate with me through the translator patiently. Then I went to the Wanda mall(万迖), shopping wasn’t quite interesting but the sun was absurdly strong. 🥵 I had an ice tea in summer break - it was quite refreshing 🍇

After few hours I decided to go back hotel and I strolled around shoshungli(所成里). Don’t expect too much in this alley - it is impressive but quite short and not so much things to buy or eat. You may better enjoy it at night after sunset. 🌅

I took break at the hotel for half an hour and went to Yan Tai mountain(煙台山). It is only 42.5 meter - only 10 minutes to arrive in the lighthouse. The ticket booth already closed, therefore I just walked around to see the coastline. 🚢

I went to chao yang street(朝陽街) where it has a history for a hundred and fifty years. It has a diverse type of buildings influenced by China, Japan, Russia, and Europe. It was the most prosperous street in the past. I found two bookstores and Zhong Shu Ge(钟書阁) was kind of Malaysian style I thought. It is painted mainly in mint color and has third floors - quite various type of books… I thought I may find one to bring back Korea but at the end bought only one bookmark illustrating this building in a square shape. 🍃

- building name: Zhong Shu Ge(钟書阁) - recommendation: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ - Achtung!: if you don't enjoy reading, it may be enough to walk first floor ;-)

There are not so many bars in this street. I just wanted to have one glass of wine but couldn’t find any appropriate. You will become familiar with starbucks on the way to Yan Tai lighthouse. Near there, there was one street bar where anyone can taste a small glass of wine made in the region. In the beginning, I started with white wine and drew the brand design in my diary. While drawing, a lady sat next to me and started talk about the draw and writing in English.

  • wine name: wonder manor (bought both suzerain and savile)
  • brewery: yantai changyu culture and tourism development co. ltd. I FINALLY MET SOMEONE I CAN TALK without feeling sorry! I invited her for sharing my wine as I was sure I cannot finish them in time. She accepted and started drinking with me! She even invited me for another wine having a taste of honey. Even if her english wasn't fluent, she at least tried to communicate with me about their culture and the plan in San Dong sheng province（山東省）.

As the wine bar closes at 9:30, we needed another plan to move on. She invited me for dinner as we both didn’t have it. I accepted and we went to Chao yang street(朝陽街) for finding the place for sea cucumber rice(海脹飯), fried fishes and mackerel dumplings. 🥟 very fresh! the restaurant name starts with xiao chao(小朝)… something! I was a bit sober so … couldn’t take any pics… wine🍷

I gave her a doll from Korea! she gave me a bag of Yan tai. I am sure we enjoyed a lot that day! It was a pleasure experience with her and hopefully to meet her again - maybe in her hometown!

4) 23rd of July in Yan Tai(煙台) and Qing Dao (請島)

I woke up in the early morning and took the taxi heading to the Yan Tai station(煙台站) - it was the lesson learnt from the first day that it is cheaper to take taxi rather than walk down all the way under the sun and being exhausted without any power to travel around.

I arrived early enough to look around the store and bought a candy. There was a small place to have breakfast before entering the station as well. I took the first class seat - just wanted to try different type of seats. First class was not so much different from second. It was clean and neat.
It took 1 hour and a half to reach Qing Dao north station(請島北站).

Once I arrived in the station, I bought the 3 days metro ticket. As a foreigner, it seems better to buy these ticket in thirty five yuan(元) rather than visiting staffs every time with the translator. In order to take the metro, you need to make your bag(包) pass via machine. It is quite simple and easy - never got something forbidden yet.

  • Achtung!: it is forbidden to eat or drink any in metro in China!

I went to Zhong Shan Lu(中山路) station taking line number one. It was quite comfortable and the hotel was just in front of the station exit B. My room was on the third floor and there was no lift. Despite of this discomfort, I would highly recommend this hotel to anyone traveling in Qing Dao!

- hotel name: Lim hotel - recommendation: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ - Achtung!: if you have big and heavy luggages, better ask staffs to assign you to the first floor.

Again the sun was absurdly strong and the humidity got really worsen. Probably due to the hangover from last night, I also got easily tired. I walked through Zhong Shan lu(中山路) and reached out the bridge of Zhanqiao(栈桥). And there were numerous tourists and I was not willing to go nearby. I took a quick picture to see later but left that bridge. I entered any restaurant nearby and ordered a dish of clams and cucumbers.. very plain but tasty 😋 I desperately desired for coffee, so I found one place called ‘THE CASH’. It has its own vibe and the taste of cake with fruits was amazing.

- cafe name: 'THE CASH' - recommendation: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ - Achtung!: you have to walk a bit to reach the location but worth it!

I sat the first floor and wrote a note of journey. And there was a lady who had started conversation with me about my diary. I was quite shy in the beginning but the way she brings up diverse topics attracted me. She was fluent in English, and good communicator. I felt so lucky that I find somebody to understand each other regardless of nationality. She and I were traveling alone and we agreed that being ourselves is the most important thing in life.

It was really lucky I met a friend who was born in different region and culture, but still consider both exploring outside and finding ourselves the most precious value in life.

5) 24th of July in Qing Dao (請島)

In the very early morning, I went to Lao Shan(嶗山). This mountain is surrounded by the coastline. We took bus 618 with one yuan(元) to reach out Taiqing scenic spot. What we did was to go all the way down till coast line and visited a village named qing shan yu cun(請山魚村). We could view the landscape of cloud, mountain, tea plants, village with a bunch of red roofs, and the coastline. I appreciated that I have a companion in this journey.

We walked all the way till the entrance of village and found one tea shop. My friend talked in Chinese with staffs and she said we can taste the tea before buying in free of charge. I learnt that it is considered polite not to drink or eat all when some are given to us in free of charge. 🙀
Drinking tea always made me feel much calmer. - then I bought two packets of tea. exciting!

We started being hungry, so entered the nearest restaurant. The view from the seat was amazing. Staffs were nice but the service was way too slow - almost took an hour to get a dish of clams, potato, and two bowls of rice. But we definitely enjoyed the view. ;-)

We reached out the bus station but did not come after a half an hour waiting. Thus, we called the taxi to get the nearest station named Dahedong(大海東). As we were so exhausted, we fell a sleep all the way till the station. I strongly recommend to visit Lao Shan in the early morning. Epsecially, if it is in July….. 🥵 You will not survive under that strong sun and humidity.

We took the line number 1 (yellow line). There are two different sections - one with strong air conditioner, another with moderate. Take the one upon your condition. Anyway we took the strong one. Many passengers in the subway seemed tired and again we all fell asleep.

After taking a break in each own’s hotel, my friend and I met at the Tai Dong station(台東站). There were a big tower in the middle of Tai Dong street. You collect a plastic cup from the nearby booth and queue one of many lines. It seems having different taste in each line. If you pay about 10~15 yuan(元), you will get a full cup of beer. Of course, you can pay with Alipay. Ours was the grapefruit flavor - and I tasted none of alcohols inside. 🍻

We had oysters with garlic on the top and a large chicken satay. Tasty and I had no stomach at all. But always better finding the place neat and clean by yourself! ‼️ I had no problem at all. The street was quite crowded - we decided to find a bar to stay at night and took a metro until Qing Dao Station. Then we walked for fifteen minutes and arrived in one of the fancy bar.

- bar name: SAMAR - recommendation: ⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️⭐️ - Achtung!: The price is quite high compared to Qing Dao beer but worthwhile to enjoy for one night - isn't it?

We ordered one glass of champagne and another of white wine(I remember it was 8~9dollars). It was still okay as we enjoyed the music, mood, and observing young chinese enjoying the night.
